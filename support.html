Support

Answers to common questions. Didn’t find yours? Write to us — we usually reply within 1–2 business days.

How do I open a PDF?

Tap Open a PDF on the home screen and pick a file on your device or in a cloud drive. You can also open a PDF from mail, a messenger or a file manager with Open with → Inkleaf or Share → Inkleaf.

Where are my changes saved?

Inkleaf keeps its own copy of each file you open, with your changes, on your device. To get the edited PDF out, use Save, Save a copy or Share. The original file stays where it was.

I removed a file from Inkleaf. Is the original deleted?

No. Remove from Inkleaf deletes only Inkleaf’s copy and its unsaved changes. The original file is not touched.

How do I undo a change?

Every change can be undone with the undo button in the editor toolbar, until you close the document.

Can I open password-protected PDFs?

Yes. Inkleaf asks for the password when you open the file. The password is used only on your device and is not stored or sent anywhere.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No. Inkleaf works fully offline and has no accounts. Your documents never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

How do I change the author name on my comments?

Tap the profile icon at the top of the home screen and set Your name. It is shown as the author of new comments.

How do I change the app language?

By default Inkleaf follows your device language. To choose another one, tap the profile icon at the top of the home screen and open Language. On Android 13+ this is the same setting as Settings → Apps → Inkleaf → Language.

A file won’t open or looks wrong

Some damaged PDFs are repaired automatically while opening. If a file still fails or renders incorrectly, please email us and, if possible, attach the file (or a version without private data) and your device model.
