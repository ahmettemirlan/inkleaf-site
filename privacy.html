Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Short version: Inkleaf does not collect, store on our servers or share any of your data. Your documents stay on your device.

Who we are

This policy covers the Inkleaf mobile app for Android and iOS (“the app”). Questions about it can be sent to chesstar96@gmail.com.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet. We do not receive your documents, your comments or any information about how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device, in its private storage:

  • copies of the PDF files you open, together with your edits and comments;
  • your list of recent and starred files and the place where you stopped reading;
  • your settings, including the author name you enter for comments.

Passwords for protected PDFs are used only to open the file and are not saved. You can delete a file’s data with Remove from Inkleaf, or all app data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Device backups made by your operating system (for example, Google or iCloud backup) may include this data according to your device settings.

Files and photos you choose

The app reads only the files and photos you explicitly pick or share with it, using the system file and photo pickers. When you save or share a document, it goes only where you choose to send it.

Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
