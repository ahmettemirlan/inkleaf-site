Features

Everything a PDF needs, nothing it doesn’t

From quick reading to real edits — in one clean app.

Read comfortably

Pick up any file exactly where you left off.

  • Smooth scrolling or one page at a time
  • Thumbnails, quick scrubber and “Go to page”
  • Search the whole document, bookmarks, night reading

Comment and mark up

Mark what matters, the way you would on paper.

  • Highlight, underline and strike through text
  • Draw in any colour, thickness and opacity
  • Shapes, arrows, text boxes and sticky notes

Edit text and images

Fix a typo or swap a picture without starting over.

  • Retype paragraphs; change font, size and colour
  • Move, resize, rotate, flip, replace or delete images
  • Add your own text and pictures anywhere

Organize pages

Put the document in the right order in seconds.

  • Reorder, rotate, duplicate and delete pages
  • Insert blank pages or pages from another PDF
  • Share just the pages you select

And a few more things

Create PDFs

Start a blank PDF or turn photos into one in a single step.

Private by design

No account, no tracking, no internet. Files stay on your device.

Protected files

Open password-protected PDFs; undo any change before saving.
