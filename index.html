Inkleaf — PDF reader & editor

A fast, focused app for reading, marking up and editing PDFs right on your phone. Works offline — your documents never leave your device.

Get it on Google Play Help & support

What you can do

Read comfortably

Smooth scrolling or page by page, thumbnails, search, bookmarks and night reading. Every file reopens where you left off.

Comment and mark up

Highlight, underline, strike out, draw, add shapes, text boxes and sticky notes.

Edit text and images

Retype paragraphs, change fonts and colours, move, resize, replace or delete images.

Organize pages

Reorder, rotate, duplicate, delete and insert pages; share only the pages you pick.

Create

New blank PDFs, or turn photos into a PDF in one step.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed. Available in 20 languages.
